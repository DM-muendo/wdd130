About Our Rafting Company

We are committed to safe, scenic, and exciting river experiences guided by experienced professionals who love the river as much as you will.

Why We Lead

Expert guides, quality gear, and clear river planning create memorable trips for every group. Each of our guides brings years of river experience, wilderness first-aid training, and a passion for sharing the thrill of white water with guests from around the world.

What Makes Us Different

We balance thrilling rapids with safety, learning, and outstanding customer care from start to finish. Every trip includes complimentary gear inspection, a thorough safety briefing, and an experienced guide who knows every bend of the river.

Our Fleet

We maintain a fleet of well-equipped rafts and support boats to handle every river condition. From nimble single-guide rafts to larger group boats with motorized support, our equipment is inspected before every trip and replaced regularly to exceed safety standards.

Our Rivers

Operating on some of the most beautiful rivers in the region, we offer trips ranging from gentle family floats to heart-pounding overnight expeditions. Each river section is carefully scouted and rated so you know exactly what to expect before you push off from shore.
