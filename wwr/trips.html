Sunrise Float

Difficulty: Easy | Duration: 3 hours

Gentle waters, breathtaking sunrise views, and a relaxed pace perfect for families and first-time rafters. Our experienced guides lead the way while you soak in the peaceful morning scenery. No prior rafting experience needed—just bring your sense of wonder.

Canyon Rush

Difficulty: Intermediate | Duration: 5 hours

More excitement, stronger rapids, and a thrilling route through scenic canyon bends. Our expert guide boat leads each group through Class III rapids, towering canyon walls, and crystal-clear pools. Ideal for adventurers who've rafted before and want the next level of challenge.

Midnight Rapids

Difficulty: Advanced | Duration: 6 hours

A high-energy challenge for experienced groups who want a memorable adventure under the stars. Navigate Class IV rapids with expert night guides, LED-equipped helmets, and the roar of the river beneath a canopy of stars. This is our signature trip for serious thrill-seekers.
